# Crossing Mechanics — Making a New Mechanism

A crossing is not "merge two ideas". It births a MECHANISM that exists in neither parent.

## Steps
1. Take ideas at the IMPOSSIBLE / WEIRD / ABSURD levels.
2. Pair them: (IMPOSSIBLE+WEIRD), (WEIRD+ABSURD), (IMPOSSIBLE+ABSURD). At least 3.
3. Ask: what EMERGES only when the two are combined? That's the new mechanism.
4. If the result is just "A and B" → fail. It must be "C that neither A nor B fully explains".

## Example
A (INVERT): guests pay to cook their own food.
B (COMPETITOR-AS-CHANNEL): the shop supplies grounds to a rival.
C: guests cook their own food, and the surplus is sold to the rival as that day's menu.
→ guest = producer & consumer; rival = distribution channel for the guests.

## Test
- Does it feel more plausible than its parent BUT still wouldn't pass a normal brainstorm? → strong candidate.
- Is there a party harmed without consent? → swap in a lawful version.

## Guardrail
Three crossings is a floor, not a cap. If none of the three yields a new mechanism, try swapping which two levels you cross before you lower your standards.
