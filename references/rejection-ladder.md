# Rejection Ladder — 5 Levels

Generate ONE idea per level, in order. Higher-level ideas must sound WRONG within the first 3 seconds.

## Level 1 — CONVENTIONAL
An existing pattern: "Uber for X". Purpose: the reference wall. Leave it raw.

## Level 2 — BORING
Add a popular trend: "AI assistant for X". Doesn't change the structure.

## Level 3 — IMPOSSIBLE
Change the industry's STRUCTURE: break 1 structural assumption. Test: "How is that even possible? That's impossible."

## Level 4 — WEIRD
Delete/invert a MANDATORY player: remove 1 core role. Test: "Why would the competitor even ...?"

## Level 5 — ABSURD
Change the relationship between players in an embarrassing/taboo way: break 2 assumptions at once. Test: "This is ridiculous" but there's hidden logic.

## /wilder escalation
BORING -> IMPOSSIBLE, IMPOSSIBLE -> WEIRD, WEIRD -> ABSURD, ABSURD -> ABSURD++.

## Note
CONVENTIONAL & BORING exist on purpose so the higher levels have contrast.

## Guardrail
Never skip CONVENTIONAL and BORING to "save time" — they anchor the contrast that makes the higher levels feel wrong.
