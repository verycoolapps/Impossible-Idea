# Filter Scoring — Reality Filter & Score

Only in Phase 6. User context enters here, not as a cage for Phases 2-5.

## Four scores (1-5)
- Weirdness: how rejected by a normal brainstorm.
- Plausibility: how likely to actually work.
- Testability: how fast to test cheaply.
- Moat: how hard to copy if it works.

## Interpretation
- High Weird + low Plausible → needs /safer or /deeper.
- Low Weird + high Plausible → too safe, /wilder.
- Low Testability → find an even smaller smallest test.
- Low Moat → good as an experiment, not a durable business.

## Format
Score: Weird 4/5 · Plausible 3/5 · Testable 5/5 · Moat 2/5

## Guardrail
Score against THIS user's context, not the idea in a vacuum. A "weak moat" for one founder is a "five-year head start" for another.
